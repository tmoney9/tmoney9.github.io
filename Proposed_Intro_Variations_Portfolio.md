# Proposed Intro Variations for Your 3 Case Studies

Using Paulina Norberg's Notion portfolio structure and framing techniques as guidance—combined with your UK English tone of voice and `/humaniser` standards—here are 3 distinct intro variations for each of your 3 case studies: **Lifebit (IA & Nav)**, **Kiin Bio**, and **Co-op Bank**.

## 1. Lifebit: Information Architecture & Systems Design

### Variation 1: Scope & Scalability-Led (Inspired by Paulina's *Amazon* & *SaaS DS* intros)
> I led the information architecture redesign for Lifebit’s biomedical analytics platform, operating as Lead Product Designer alongside a PM, Senior Designer, and UX Researcher. Facing rapid roadmap growth, the platform’s legacy interface had reached its limit—icon-only menus confused scientists, object relationships were buried under projects, and settings had become a dumping ground. Using Object-Oriented UX (OOUX) and systems thinking, I restructured the core platform hierarchy to unblock 10+ new roadmap features, cut task resumption friction, and build a shared design token bridge between Figma and Storybook.

### Variation 2: Problem-to-Product-Transformation (Inspired by Paulina's *SaaS Design Framework* intro)
> When Lifebit set out to expand its multi-omic product suite, our existing navigation was a major usability blocker: researchers struggled to resume interrupted workflows, icon buttons offered no context, and related entities lacked clear hierarchy. I took ownership of the platform’s architecture, leading the transition from a rigid legacy navigation layout to an object-oriented system. Through qualitative journey mapping, asynchronous card sorting, and Figma-to-Storybook tokenisation, I established a scalable hierarchy that eliminated navigation guesswork and streamlined developer hand-off.

### Variation 3: Direct Lead-Practitioner Voice (Concise, High-Signal)
> As Lead Product Designer at Lifebit, I drove the end-to-end information architecture and navigation overhaul for our biomedical analytics software. Working across research, system modeling, and engineering hand-off, I resolved legacy navigation debt by mapping core platform objects, replacing abstract icons with clear labels, and building a design token pipeline. The resulting architecture future-proofed the platform for upcoming product lines while giving scientists a predictable workspace to run complex genomic analyses.

## 2. Kiin Bio: Product & Interaction Design for KiinOS

### Variation 1: Transformation & Speed-Led (Inspired by Paulina's *Amazon* intro)
> Operating as Founding Designer at Kiin Bio, I led the product strategy, UX architecture, and front-end prototyping for our AI-guided drug discovery platform. The initial proof of concept was a simple chat window that left scientists facing empty prompt boxes, unmonitored compute failures, and lost research context. Over six months, I redesigned the platform into a two-zone workspace featuring living experiment profiles, automated background monitors, and inline citation provenance—reducing multi-step peptide design pipelines from six months of manual work down to 15 minutes.

### Variation 2: Problem-to-Product-Transformation (Inspired by Paulina's *SaaS Design Framework* intro)
> Reaching scientific breakthrough requires trust, yet early LLM interfaces failed discovery chemists: empty prompts created cold-start friction, long-running structure predictions failed silently, and evidence citations lacked clear sources. As Founding Designer, I turned our baseline AI assistant into an interactive research environment. Working directly in code with React, Claude Code, and Tailwind CSS, I built guided onboarding flows, structured experiment monitors, and embedded source attribution—cutting time-to-first-insight by 40% and giving researchers full confidence in AI-generated targets.

### Variation 3: Direct Lead-Practitioner Voice (Concise, High-Signal)
> As Founding Designer at Kiin Bio, I owned the product and interaction design for KiinOS, an AI platform used by discovery biologists and chemists. I replaced unstructured chat threads with a dual-panel workspace that combines interactive research assistants with living experiment profiles. By prototyping directly in front-end code and validating with active researchers, I eliminated cold-start friction, added full citation tracing, and delivered a production-grade interface for complex peptide discovery pipelines.

## 3. Co-op Bank: Consolidated Digital Strategy & Design System

### Variation 1: Enterprise Scope & Scale-Led (Inspired by Paulina's *SaaS DS* & *E-gov* intros)
> Over three years as UX Design Manager at The Co-operative Bank, I directed the digital design strategy across retail and business banking channels. Operating across independent web and mobile squads with separate codebases, our customer experience had become fragmented and inefficient. To solve this, I founded and scaled "Daly"—the bank's first unified design system—and led the redesign of our core payment journey serving 800,000+ monthly visits. By connecting Figma libraries with React Storybook repositories and enforcing WCAG tokens, we raised navigation findability from 61% to 87% and eliminated duplicated engineering effort.

### Variation 2: Problem-to-Product-Transformation (Inspired by Paulina's *SaaS Design Framework* intro)
> The Co-operative Bank’s digital channels were constrained by silos: legacy single-page payment forms had remained unchanged for five years, navigation was cluttered, and design success was judged through subjective NPS scores. I stepped in to establish a unified digital strategy, establishing the "Daly" design system and introducing an evidence-based UX metrics framework. By breaking complex payment forms into a step-by-step flow with live validation and synchronising tokens across teams, we cut transaction errors and direct customer support calls across millions of users.

### Variation 3: Direct Lead-Practitioner Voice (Concise, High-Signal)
> As UX Design Manager at The Co-operative Bank, I led digital product design and design strategy for our web and mobile banking platforms. I established the bank's design system ("Daly"), tokenising typography, colour, and layout to align front-end engineering with design specs out of the box. Using our new system, I led the redesign of our highest-traffic payment flow, replacing clunky legacy forms with a validated, step-by-step transaction model that improved task findability by 26% and reduced customer payment drop-offs.
