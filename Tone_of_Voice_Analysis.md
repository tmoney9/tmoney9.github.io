# Tone of Voice & Copywriting Analysis — shanef.xyz

An analysis of the tone of voice, copy style, and brand positioning across your portfolio site ([shanef.xyz](https://shanef.xyz/)).

---

## Executive Summary

Your site communicates with a **Pragmatic, Senior-Level Engineering-Design Tone**. It avoids fluff, visual buzzwords, and vague UX jargon, preferring **action-oriented language, clear technical specs, and concrete outcomes**.

You sound like a **hands-on lead practitioner** who bridging design, product strategy, and front-end code (Design Engineering).

---

## Key Tone Dimensions & Pillars

```
Pragmatic / Grounded <--------------------o> Overly Hype / Academic
Direct / Technical    <------------------o-> Generic Consumer UX
Warm / Human          <------------o------> Cold / Corporate Spec
Confident / Modest    <-----------------o-> Arrogant / Self-Aggrandising
```

### 1. Pragmatic & Outcome-Driven (Primary Pillar)
- **Characteristics:** Focused on what was actually built, who used it, and what problem it solved.
- **Evidence:** 
  - *"Scientists executed multi-step peptide design pipelines in 15 minutes that previously took 6 months of manual work."*
  - *"Tree-testing task success rose from 61% to 87% under the flattened, mobile-first navigation structure."*
- **Effect:** Immediately builds trust with hiring managers, PMs, and engineering leaders because you speak in business and workflow impact rather than aesthetic preferences.

### 2. Technical & Craft-Focused (Secondary Pillar)
- **Characteristics:** You explicitly name technologies, frameworks, and methodologies (*OOUX*, *OMOP CDM*, *Claude Code*, *Storybook*, *Tokenisation*, *React*, *Tailwind*).
- **Evidence:**
  - *"Synchronised Figma component libraries with React Storybook repositories..."*
  - *"Built high-fidelity front-end prototypes using Claude Code, React, and Tailwind CSS..."*
- **Effect:** Positions you clearly as a **Design Engineer / Systems Lead** who understands technical constraints and developer hand-off, not just a surface-level UI designer.

### 3. Direct & Unpretentious (Personality)
- **Characteristics:** Active voice, short declarative sentences, minimal corporate filler words (*"synergy"*, *"delight"*, *"holistic transformation"* are completely absent).
- **Evidence:**
  - *"I spend my off-hours working on DIY home projects and visiting National Trust sites."*
  - *"I can help with: Digital product design, Design systems at scale..."*
- **Effect:** Highly readable and authentic. The personal touch (DIY projects, National Trust) adds a relaxed, approachable warmth that balances the dense technical domain topics (Genomics, AI, FinTech).

## Tone Archetype: *The Systems Architect Who Builds*

If we summarize your tone into a single persona, it is **"The Systems Architect Who Builds"**:

1. **You don't just design screens; you design systems** (*OOUX, Tokenisation, Frameworks*).
2. **You don't just hand off Figma links; you prototype in code** (*React, Tailwind, Storybook*).
3. **You don't guess outcome success; you measure it** (*Treejack, Adobe Analytics, PostHog*).

---

## Recommendations to Elevate the Tone Further

1. **Maintain the Active Voice in Case Studies:**
   - Keep leading sentences subject-first (*"We eliminated..."* / *"I built..."*) rather than passive voice (*"Elimination of..."*).
2. **Harmonise Headers Across Case Studies:**
   - Continue using consistent 1–2 word section headers (`Impact`, `Challenges`, `Project details`, `Results`) across all project pages so the site feels unified.
3. **Capitalize on the "Design Engineer" Hybrid Positioning:**
   - Your current tone bridges UX strategy and code very well. Keep explicitly highlighting your use of modern AI tooling (like *Claude Code*) as it sets you apart from traditional UX designers.
