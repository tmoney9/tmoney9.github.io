# Style Guide & Tone Guidelines (Shane Fadden Portfolio)

All case study rewrites, descriptions, and copy edits across this portfolio must strictly follow this Tone of Voice & Writing Style Guide.

---

## 1. Tone Archetype: "The Systems Architect Who Builds"

- **Pragmatic & Outcome-Driven:** Lead with real product outcomes, workflow time saved, or business metrics. Avoid vague claims.
- **Technical & Craft-Focused:** Speak comfortably about design tokens, component APIs, OOUX, Storybook, React, Tailwind, and AI interfaces.
- **Direct & Unpretentious:** Use active voice, simple verbs, and clear sentences. Eliminate corporate jargon.

---

## 2. Strict Anti-AI Smell Rules (/humaniser)

To ensure copy sounds natural, human, and authentic, **NEVER use the following AI cliché words and phrases**:

### Banned Words & Phrases
- **Overused AI Buzzwords:** *delve, testament, beacon, paramount, tapestry, landscape, game-changer, seamless, cornerstone, pivotal, holistic, fostering, leveraging, orchestrating, transformative, realm, spearhead, spearheading, robust, cutting-edge, vibrant, paradigm, synergy.*
- **AI Sentence Formulas:**
  - *"In today's fast-paced digital world..."*
  - *"It wasn't just about X, it was about Y..."* (Use sparingly, avoid repeating across pages)
  - *"By doing X, we ensured Y..."*
  - *"A testament to..."*
  - *"A deep dive into..."*

### Natural Human Writing Rules
1. **Be Specific Over Generic:** Replace *"seamless experience"* with *"cut form drop-offs"* or *"eliminated silent compute errors"*.
2. **Vary Sentence Length:** Mix punchy 5-word statements with longer explanatory sentences.
3. **Use Concrete Action Verbs:** *built, designed, tested, shipped, cut, audited, mapped, structured, unified, benchmarked*.
4. **First-Person Grounded Tone:** Write naturally in the first person (*"I built"*, *"We ran"*) or concise imperative/past-tense bullet points.
